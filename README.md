# Your website

Everything for your site lives in this folder: **website**, in your home folder.

## See it on your computer
1. Open the Terminal app.
2. Type `bash ~/website/preview.sh` and press Return.
3. Open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
4. When you're done, go back to Terminal and press Ctrl+C.

## Change something
Open an AI agent (such as Claude Code) in this folder and say, in plain words, what you want, for example:
"Read AGENTS.md and help me update my website. Add my new paper to the Publications page."
The agent will show you the change on your computer before anything goes online.
