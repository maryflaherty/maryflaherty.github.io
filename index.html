Mary M. Flaherty

I study how listening experience shapes children’s ability to hear in a noisy world.

I’m an Associate Professor of Speech and Hearing Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I direct the Child Speech Research Lab. When I’m not teaching or in the lab, you’ll find me at a live show, deep in a book, or making art (like the painting here).

Research

Listening in the noise

A teacher talking, classmates whispering, chairs scraping, a heater humming. Picking one voice out of that mix is hard work, and harder for children than for adults. My lab studies what helps, and what gets in the way.

Learning to ask “How do we know?”

More than anything, I want my students to leave as critical consumers of information: people who can find evidence, weigh it, and make good decisions with it.
